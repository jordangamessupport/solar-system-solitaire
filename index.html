Frequently asked questions

How does a voyage work?

Fly from the Sun to Neptune one game of Klondike at a time. Every move uses fuel. Reach the target score, then tap your ship to land and move on to the next planet. Clear the whole board to earn an upgrade for your ship. Each planet has its own condition, shown before the game starts. How to Play in the game has the full guide.

I bought Remove Ads, but I'm still seeing ads.

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Restoring is free.

How do I get Remove Ads on a new iPhone?

Install the game, sign in with the same Apple Account, then tap Restore Purchases in Settings.

When does the Daily Challenge reset?

A new Daily Challenge starts every day at midnight UTC (7 pm Central Daylight Time, 6 pm Central Standard Time). Every player gets the same deal. You can play days you missed from the calendar.

Can every deal be won?

Before each game, the game searches for a deal that can be cleared. In rare cases the check can't finish in time and you get a regular shuffle instead.

My progress disappeared.

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress, and it doesn't carry over to a new phone. Leaderboard scores are kept by Game Center.

The leaderboard isn't showing up.

Leaderboards use Game Center. Check that you're signed in under Settings › Game Center on your iPhone and that you're connected to the internet.

How do I turn off sound?

Open Settings in the game and tap Sound. Your iPhone's silent switch also mutes the game.
